# Act 2 music: prompts for an AI music generator (Suno or similar)

Seven songs for the Sunscar desert. Each one has a **title**, the **file name** the game expects, a **style prompt**
(paste into "Style of Music"), what to **exclude**, and the **lyrics / structure** box (paste into "Lyrics").
Turn on **Instrumental** for every song except the main theme (which has an optional sung version).

Same family as Act 1 (Hearth of Oakvale, Blob Slayer, the Pale Marshal's March): dark fantasy RPG score, live-sounding
instruments, warm and a little weathered, not cinematic-trailer bombast. What changes in the desert: Middle-Eastern
and North-African colours (oud, ney flute, qanun, duduk, frame drums, darbuka, hand claps), modes like Hijaz and
Phrygian dominant, heat, distance, sand.

When a song is ready: download the mp3, name it exactly as below, and send it in the project. The game already has a
slot for each one: it starts playing in the right place as soon as the file is added (until then the desert plays
the desert sounds made in code, and the Act 1 battle songs in fights).

| Song | File | Where it plays |
|---|---|---|
| Song of the Second Sun | `song-of-the-second-sun.mp3` | Main theme: once, the first time you step through the waygate |
| Kel Ashar tune | `kel-ashar-tune.mp3` | Calm, in Kel Ashar (desert sounds under it) |
| Dune tune | `dune-tune.mp3` | Calm, the open desert (dunes, flats, mesas, Thornspire) |
| Oasis tune | `oasis-tune.mp3` | Calm, at Palmshade Oasis, Mirage Wells, Jackal Springs |
| The Buried City | `the-buried-city.mp3` | Calm, in Kharsun and the Veiled Sanctum |
| Sand and Steel | `sand-and-steel.mp3` | Battle anywhere in the desert (plays once, swells with the fight) |
| Ishael, the Sunwright | `ishael-the-sunwright.mp3` | Boss fight at the Sun Furnace (loops while the fight lasts) |

Tips: generate 3 to 4 versions of each and keep the best. Calm tunes should loop well, so pick one whose end is
quiet. Battle and boss songs: 2:30 to 3:30.

---

## 1. Song of the Second Sun (Act 2 main theme)

**Style prompt**
```
epic desert fantasy RPG main theme, Hijaz mode, solo oud opening over low drone, ney flute melody, frame drums and darbuka build, string orchestra swells, wordless female vocal in the climax, warm, ancient, mysterious, slow heat, 80 bpm rising to 100 bpm
```
**Exclude**
```
electronic, EDM, synth bass, trap drums, pop vocals, guitar solo
```
**Lyrics (instrumental version)**
```
[Intro: solo oud, distant wind, low drone]
[Verse: ney flute takes the melody, soft frame drum]
[Build: darbuka and hand claps enter, strings rise]
[Chorus: full orchestra, wordless female vocal, the main melody, big and warm]
[Bridge: everything drops, lone duduk, heat shimmer]
[Final Chorus: full ensemble, choir, drums]
[Outro: oud alone, fades into the wind]
```
**Lyrics (optional sung version, turn Instrumental off)**
```
[Intro: solo oud, distant wind]

[Verse]
Through the old gate, out of the green
Into the land that the sun burned clean
Kharsun sleeps where the river ran
Under the sand, under the hand

[Pre-Chorus]
The wells are sinking, a finger a day
The veiled ones whisper the old words away

[Chorus]
One sun to rise, one sun to fall
No second fire above us all
Hold the water, hold the flame
The Sunscar remembers your name

[Bridge: duduk solo, heat shimmer]

[Final Chorus]
One sun to rise, one sun to fall
No second fire above us all

[Outro: oud alone, wind]
```

## 7. Ishael, the Sunwright (Act 2 final boss)

Ishael leads the Veiled Circle, sun-priests who want to light a second sun over the desert. She fights at the Sun
Furnace on top of the Thornspire, with a choir of adepts; her phases are "the Choir" and "the Second Sun".

**Style prompt**
```
epic final boss battle music, dark fantasy RPG, desert sun cult, huge choir chanting, pipe organ and blazing brass, thundering taiko and darbuka, fast oud and qanun runs in Hijaz mode, soaring female soprano, burning, apocalyptic, 150 bpm, intense
```
**Exclude**
```
electronic, dubstep, EDM, pop, rock guitar, rap
```
**Lyrics**
```
[Intro: sun-gong hit, choir holds one burning note]
[Choir chant, low and menacing]
Ishael! Ishael!
Kindle the second sun!

[Main: taiko, darbuka, fast oud riff, brass]

[Phase two: soaring female soprano over the choir]
Burn the wells, drink the rivers
Light the sky a second time

[Climax: full choir, organ, brass, drums]
Ishael! Ishael!
Kindle the second sun!

[Break: drums only, heartbeat]
[Final: everything, faster, louder]
[Outro: one huge choir chord, cut to silence]
```
