# Songweaver: progress

New class: the Songweaver, an Oakvale minstrel with a lute. Work follows the orchestrated agent flow in
[CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) ("Working with agents"), starting with the planner. QA only on request.

Status: **planned, waiting on the owner's answers** (2026-11-13). No code yet.

## Decisions (2026-11-13)
- Songweaver, an **Oakvale minstrel** who plays a **lute**.
- **Support, but fully viable solo:** not pure support, it must deal real damage of its own.
- **PvP healing and buffs: uncapped.**
- Becomes an active class (`ACTIVE_CLASSES` in `shared/classes.ts`).

- (owner, 2026-11-13) **The skill trees are built around musical concepts:** unlocking new keys, musical styles and
  similar. The general feel matters most to the owner.
- (owner, 2026-11-13) **Balance is the orchestrator's call** (the targets and gates in open questions 3 and 6).
  The planner's tree below is being redesigned to fit.

- (owner, 2026-11-13) **Monsters differ in sensitivity to sound.** Music can **bounce** and **resonate**: both
  become real mechanics.

## Design starting point
The planner refines this and lists open questions.
- **Songs are auras:** one song active at a time, affecting the Songweaver and nearby party members.
- **Three branches:**
  - **Hearthsongs:** healing over time and shields for allies.
  - **Warsongs:** party speed, damage and poise buffs.
  - **Discord:** enemy slows, weakening, poise drain, and the class's main solo damage.
- **Chords:** casting songs or notes in a certain order triggers a finisher. Based on order, not timing, so network
  delay doesn't matter.
- **Sound:** each skill plays real lute notes through `client/src/sfx.ts`, all in one shared key, so a party sounds
  musical together.
- **Basic attack:** a plucked-string sound wave or a lute strike (planner proposes which).

## Known engine work
Heals and buffs only reach the caster today. Effects must be able to reach allies (party members) in
`shared/skillEngine.ts`. Design it so other classes can reuse it.

## Constraints (see CLAUDE.md)
- The `Player` schema is at its 64-field cap: class state must be packed into existing fields.
- Skill-tree branches at most 3 deep.
- Art is generated by code: hero sheet, lute, skill icons, effects.
- Balance: duel and PvE sims against Wizard, Vampire and Summoner, at every level band up to 50.
- Patch note.

## Open questions for the planner
- How exactly chords are triggered, and what each one does.
- Whether songs from several Songweavers can affect the same player at once.
- How strong solo damage should be compared with the Wizard (already the weakest class).

## Plan v2 (planner, 2026-11-13). Music-first; APPROVED by the owner 2026-11-13
This replaces the v1 tree. The v1 engine ally design (`party`, `shareBuff`, shields, poise) still stands.

**Concept:** progression is music.
- Lv1: one major village tune.
- First Discord point: unlocks Minor.
- Lv 8–12: each branch unlocks a mode (Lydian, Dorian, Phrygian) and a device (held note, tempo, sympathetic
  strings, crescendo).
- The `sg_modulate` cantrip switches mode in combat. Major favours allies, Minor and Phrygian favour damage, Dorian
  is neutral.
- Sound is physical: waves bounce off stone and die in wood, monsters hear differently, and right pitches and
  intervals build resonance until it bursts.

**Tree:** 6 skills per branch, at most 3 deep. Each skill has a degree [I/ii/♭II/IV/V/vi/i] and a pitch
(Low/Mid/High).
- Hymnal: Hearth Ballad [I] (regen song), Mending Note [IV] (heal that chains to an ally), Pedal Point [vi] (party
  shield), Lydian (overheal becomes a shield), Legato (buffs linger), Chorale [I] (ultimate heal that grows with the
  number of party members inside).
- Folk: Marching Tune [I] (speed and poise), Jig [ii] (attack speed), Call [V] (call-and-response damage buff in a
  4s window), Dorian (+radius, both colours), Accelerando (song ticks speed up with Tempo), Anthem [I] (crescendo
  ultimate).
- Discord: Dirge [i] (slow, poise drain; unlocks Minor), Shrill Note [V] (piercing shot that bounces off walls,
  1→3 bounces), Cracked String [♭II] (stun; a canon echo from rank 3), Phrygian (+poise drain, +resonance),
  Sympathetic Strings (+resonance; bursts spread), Twelve-Tone Storm (ultimate that matches every pitch).

**Chords:** real progressions that fire automatically on the 3rd note, then a 12s lockout. The mode colours each
finisher.

| Progression | Order | Finisher |
|---|---|---|
| Amen | vi–IV–I | heal and shield |
| Turnaround | ii–V–I | party damage and attack speed buff |
| Neapolitan | ♭II–V–i | Shatterchord: damage, stun, +50 resonance |
| Perfect | IV–V–I | heal and damage, +30 resonance |
| Deceptive | IV–V–vi | untouchable, fear |

**Key:** one tonic, A. Modes recolour it; the server sends the degree and mode, and the client sets the pitch.
Songweavers in different modes are voiced as open fifths so they stay in tune together.

**Sound sensitivity:** a `sound: { tier, pitch?, mode? }` field in every `MOB_COMBAT` entry.
- Tiers: deaf, dull, normal, keen, fragile. They scale damage, slow, poise drain and resonance; fragile shatters.
- A matching pitch gives ×1.2 damage and ×1.5 resonance. A mode affinity (undead→major, beasts/fey→minor) gives
  ×1.3 resonance.
- Players learn it from tinted numbers, hit sounds, a nameplate ear icon and a one-time hint line.
- Heroes are normal; the Songweaver has Trained Ear (½ resonance on herself).
- Only Songweaver sound hits count.

**Bounce:**
- Walls: in `updateProjectile`, 2 extra `isSolidAt` lookups find the axis to reflect on. Max 3 bounces, 24 px
  minimum flight between bounces.
- Material falloff: stone ×0.9, wood ×0.6, well/anvil ×1.0 (+resonance), foliage absorbs.
- Chains reuse `ShotDef.chain`: 70 px, no repeat targets.

**Resonance:**
- A 0–100 counter, server-only in `SkillRuntime`.
- An interval bonus for a fifth or an octave.
- Decay after 2s.
- A burst at 100, at most 1 per target per second.
- A spread alone never bursts.
- Shared between Songweavers.
- Shown as `res1`–`res3` in the existing `fx` string, plus a `resonate` sfx event. No schema change.

**Acoustics:** `shared/acoustics.ts` builds a deterministic material grid. Song modifiers come from tiles near the
caster: water slows longer, a well widens the radius, stone boosts low notes. A bell object would come later.

**New files:** `shared/songs.ts`, `shared/acoustics.ts`, `server/songs.ts`; save fields `swTree`, `swMode`.

**Risks:**
- The ally/enemy power split from modes touches every class's casts.
- The 1D sims can't bounce, so they use an expected-value factor.
- Deaf blobs slow down early Discord play.
- Golems get a sensitivity, which changes Songweaver vs Summoner duels.
- Chorale plus uncapped PvP healing.

**Baseline before the change** (n=60 duel / n=20 PvE, noisy):
- Wizard duel win rate 2/38/35/23/39/42/42/51/53% at Lv 1/5/10/15/20/25/30/40/50.
- The Vampire, not the Wizard, is the weakest at Lv 40–50 (17–20%).
- PvE seconds per kill: Wizard 2–4s, Vampire 1.5–2.6s, Summoner 2.7–4.3s.

## Owner decisions on the open questions (2026-11-13): all 15 recommendations accepted as written
1. Chord trigger: automatic (vs. a Finale key).
2. Stacking: different songs stack, the same song doesn't; resonance on a monster is shared.
3. Key: one tonic, A, with open fifths when modes clash (vs. a party conductor).
4. Modes of one tonic presented as "keys" (vs. real keys on the circle of fifths).
5. Heroes are normal; Trained Ear on the Songweaver; `soundRes` gear later.
6. Keep mode affinities on monsters (vs. pitch only).
7. Only Songweaver hits build resonance.
8. Waves pass through allies; only Mending Note chains between allies.
9. Resonant objects: existing tiles in v1, a bell later.
10. Minor unlocks with Dirge.
11. A 4s Call window is acceptable.
12. Oakvale: major-mode Hymnal and Folk songs are allowed (busking); minor modes and Discord make you an outlaw.
13. Starter lute only in v1; tuned lutes later.
14. 30s songs, recoloured by Modulate.
15. `swTree = 1` and `swMode` from the first release.

Balance targets are the orchestrator's call (owner, 2026-11-13).

## Interface contract (orchestrator, 2026-11-13)
Agents build against this in parallel. Changing it needs the orchestrator.
- **Class and skill ids:**
  - Class `songweaver`.
  - Skills: `sg_ballad sg_mend sg_pedal sg_lydian sg_legato sg_chorale` · `sg_march sg_jig sg_call sg_dorian sg_accel sg_anthem` ·
    `sg_dirge sg_shrill sg_crack sg_phrygian sg_sympathy sg_twelve`, plus the cantrip `sg_modulate`.
  - Finishers (hidden skills): `sg_f_amen sg_f_encore sg_f_shatter sg_f_resolve sg_f_sleight`.
- **Values:**
  - Modes `major minor lydian dorian phrygian`.
  - Degrees `I i ii bII IV V vi`.
  - Pitch `L M H any`.
  - Sound tiers `deaf dull normal keen fragile`.
  - Tonic A (MIDI 45 = A2); the client maps degree + mode to pitch.
- **`broadcast("sfx", …)` events:**
  - `{ ev: "note", id, s, deg, mode, pitch, x, y }`: every Songweaver skill cast and the basic-attack combo step.
  - `{ ev: "chord", id, prog, mode, x, y }`: a finisher fired; `prog` is `amen | encore | shatter | resolve | sleight`.
  - `{ ev: "resonate", id, x, y, shatter }`: a resonance burst.
  - `{ ev: "bounce", x, y, mat }`: a wave bounces off a wall; `mat` is `stone | wood | metal`.
  - `{ ev: "soundhit", id, x, y, tier, match }`: a sound hit on a monster, used for the ringing/muffled cue.
  - `{ ev: "shieldbreak", id, s, x, y }`: already exists.
- **Status words** in the existing `fx` string: `mode:<id>`, `res1` / `res2` / `res3`; buff ids as today.
- **Owner-only message:** `"chord"` with `{ notes: Degree[], mode, lockMs }`, sent when it changes.
- **Projectile kind:** `"wave"` (basic attack and Shrill Note), drawn with `client/src/soundWaveArt.ts`.
