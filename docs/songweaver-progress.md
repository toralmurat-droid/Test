# Songweaver: progress

New class: the Songweaver, an Oakvale minstrel with a lute. Work follows the orchestrated agent flow in
[CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) ("Working with agents"), starting with the planner. QA only on request.

Status: **brief only**. No spec, plan or code yet.

## Decisions (2026-11-13)
- Songweaver, an **Oakvale minstrel** who plays a **lute**.
- **Support, but fully viable solo:** not pure support, it must deal real damage of its own.
- **PvP healing and buffs: uncapped.**
- Becomes an active class (`ACTIVE_CLASSES` in `shared/classes.ts`).

## Design starting point
The planner refines this and lists open questions.
- **Songs are auras:** one song active at a time, affecting the Songweaver and nearby party members.
- **Three branches:**
  - **Hearthsongs:** healing over time and shields for allies.
  - **Warsongs:** party speed, damage and poise buffs.
  - **Discord:** enemy slows, weakening, poise drain, and the class's main solo damage.
- **Chords:** casting songs or notes in a certain order triggers a finisher. Based on order, not timing, so network
  delay doesn't matter.
- **Sound:** each skill plays real lute notes through `client/src/sfx.ts`, all in one shared key, so a party sounds
  musical together.
- **Basic attack:** a plucked-string sound wave or a lute strike (planner proposes which).

## Known engine work
Heals and buffs only reach the caster today. Effects must be able to reach allies (party members) in
`shared/skillEngine.ts`. Design it so other classes can reuse it.

## Constraints (see CLAUDE.md)
- The `Player` schema is at its 64-field cap: class state must be packed into existing fields.
- Skill-tree branches at most 3 deep.
- Art is generated by code: hero sheet, lute, skill icons, effects.
- Balance: duel and PvE sims against Wizard, Vampire and Summoner, at every level band up to 50.
- Patch note.

## Open questions for the planner
- How exactly chords are triggered, and what each one does.
- Whether songs from several Songweavers can affect the same player at once.
- How strong solo damage should be compared with the Wizard (already the weakest class).
